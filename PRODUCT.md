# Barça Fan Platform — Product Truth

The platform is a premium, dark blue-and-garnet fan game for FC Barcelona supporters. Its core loop is: make a verifiable football prediction, wait for the real outcome, receive XP in a relevant skill, compare progress, and return for the next match.

## Product principles

- The complete football game remains free: matches, score and lineup predictions, Fantasy, transfers, challenges, duels, analytics, community and leaderboards.
- XP is earned only for confirmed football outcomes. Comments, likes, clicks and purchases never grant XP.
- A wrong prediction does not remove XP; it can lower a skill accuracy percentage.
- The account level aggregates specialist tracks: Results, Exact Score, Tactics, Transfers, Fantasy, Duels, Knowledge, Analyst and Scout.
- Premium is identity, depth, convenience and community—not pay-to-win.

## Local release mode

Supabase and real payments are intentionally disabled for this iteration. Fantasy teams, memberships, VIP votes and digital purchases persist in localStorage and are shaped for later migration.

## Memberships

- Culé — 499 ₽ / month
- Culé Pro — 1,490 ₽ / month
- Socio 1899 — 3,990 ₽ / month

Socio 1899 unlocks a private club: Matchroom, Transfer War Room, VIP league, closed club discussions, VIP Council votes, numbered monthly digital drops, personal analytics and scheduled live events. No copyrighted match video is streamed.

## Digital shop

Only digital cosmetics and utilities are sold in the first release: profile frames, sticker packs, reactions, tactical-board themes, poster/export templates and interface animations. Physical merchandise is outside the current scope.
